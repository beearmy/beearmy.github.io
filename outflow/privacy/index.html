Privacy Policy

Effective 12 June 2026

Outflow does not collect any data. None.

Everything you enter in Outflow — subscriptions, prices, notes, imported receipts — is stored only on your device. Outflow has no server, no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. The app makes no network connections to us, because there is no "us" infrastructure to connect to.

On-device intelligence

Smart Paste (text, photo and PDF reading) runs entirely on your iPhone using Apple's on-device frameworks. The content you import is never transmitted anywhere.

Purchases

The optional Pro unlock is processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles purchases.

Notifications

Renewal reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device.

Your data, your control

Deleting the app deletes its data. You can export your subscriptions to CSV at any time (Pro).

Changes & contact

Any future change to this policy will be posted on this page. Questions: contact us via a support request.
