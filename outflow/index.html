Outflow: Subscription Tracker

Know exactly what you pay for. Outflow is a privacy-first subscription tracker for iPhone: track every recurring cost, get reminders before renewals, and see where the money goes — with no bank linking, no account, and nothing ever leaving your device.

  • 🔒 Private by design — everything stays on your iPhone
  • ✨ Smart Paste — paste a receipt, or pick a screenshot or PDF, and on-device intelligence fills in the details
  • 🔔 Renewal reminders with clear monthly & yearly totals
  • 💜 Fair pricing — free for up to 5 subscriptions; Pro is a one-time purchase, never a subscription
