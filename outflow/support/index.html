Common questions

  • Does Outflow connect to my bank? No — never. You add subscriptions yourself, or let Smart Paste read a receipt on-device.
  • Is my data uploaded anywhere? No. Everything is stored on your iPhone. There's no account and no analytics.
  • Does deleting a subscription in Outflow cancel the real subscription? No — Outflow tracks; cancelling happens with the service itself.
  • Is Pro a subscription? No. Pro is a single one-time purchase, yours forever, restorable on any of your devices.
