Landlord Assistant: Rent

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 June 2026

Overview

Landlord Assistant: Rent is designed to keep landlord, property, lodger, rent, and paperwork records on your device. The app does not sell personal data and does not use third-party advertising SDKs.

Information You Enter

You may choose to add property details, lodger names, contact details, rent records, checklist items, notes, and document attachments. This information is stored locally on your device unless you enable optional iCloud sync.

iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud sync, an encrypted app snapshot is stored in your Apple iCloud account so your own devices can share the same records. Apple handles iCloud account security and storage. The app developer does not operate a separate server for those records.

Sign in with Apple

If you use Sign in with Apple, the app may store your Apple user identifier and, if Apple provides them, your display name and email address. These are used only to show account status inside the app.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. The app receives purchase entitlement status from StoreKit so it can unlock paid features. Payment details are handled by Apple and are not received by the app developer.

Support

If you contact support through GitHub or email, the information you choose to include in that message will be used to respond to your request.

Data Removal

You can delete app records from inside the app settings. You can also delete the app to remove local app data from the device. iCloud data can be removed from the app settings when iCloud sync is enabled.

Contact

For privacy questions, open a support issue on the AndyHQ tracker. Tickets are labelled for Landlord Assistant: Rent.
