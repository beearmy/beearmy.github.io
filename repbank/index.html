Learn one exercise a day. Bank it for life.

Rep Bank is for people who avoid the gym because they're scared of doing it wrong. Like Duolingo — but instead of words, you learn exercises: proper form first, one movement at a time, starting from the easiest.

  • 📸 See it done properly. Every exercise has a photo walkthrough, plain-English setup steps and coach's tips — no jargon, no guesswork.
  • 🔥 Ten days to mastery. Practise one exercise daily. Ten days banks it for life and unlocks the next one on your path.
  • 🧘 Rest days are sacred. Planned rest never breaks a streak. Rep Bank celebrates recovery instead of guilting you.
  • 🏠 Gym or living room. Tell it what equipment you've got today — a wall and a chair are enough to keep progressing.
  • 🔒 Private by design. No accounts. Your progress stays on your device and in your own iCloud.

Why it works

Most fitness apps assume you already know what a "lat pulldown" is. Rep Bank assumes the opposite: that walking into a gym feels intimidating, and that confidence comes from knowing you can do one thing properly. So it teaches exactly that — one movement, mastered, then the next. Your bank only ever grows.

FAQ

Do I need a gym membership?

No. Whole paths are bodyweight-only. Equipment-based exercises tell you exactly what they need before you start.

What if I miss a day?

Mark rest days in advance and your streak is safe. Life happens — Rep Bank is built to be forgiving, not punishing.

Is it really private?

Yes — no accounts, no tracking SDKs, no ads. Progress syncs through your own private iCloud and nowhere else. Read the privacy policy.
