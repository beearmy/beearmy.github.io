Privacy policy

Matched Bets Calculator is designed to keep your personal records under your control.

Effective 13 October 2026 · Developer: AndyHQ / Andy Mesa

What the app stores

The app stores the information you enter: events, selections, markets, bookmakers and exchanges, currency, stakes, odds, commission, matched lay entries, dates, notes, links between bets and settlement amounts. It also keeps revisions so you can correct records and recover reversible deletions.

Calculations run on your device. The app works without a separate app account, bookmaker login or live-odds connection. We do not receive your bet records through an app analytics or advertising service.

Advertising and analytics

The app contains no advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK or tracking across other companies’ apps and websites. It does not request access to your advertising identifier. Local preferences and review-prompt eligibility are stored on your device.

Private iCloud

Production iCloud setup is still in preparation for the internal beta. Local saving remains available. When private iCloud sync is available and your device is signed in to iCloud, records are stored in your own private CloudKit database through Apple. The developer cannot browse those private records in CloudKit Console. Apple’s account, storage and privacy terms apply. Sync is not a substitute for an independent backup.

Device authentication and widgets

Optional app lock uses Apple’s device authentication, including Face ID, Touch ID or device passcode where available. The app does not receive your biometric information. It conceals sensitive content when inactive. Widgets read a small summary in the app’s protected shared container; financial amounts are hidden by default and concealed when app lock is enabled.

Reminders and Shortcuts

Reminders are optional local notifications, scheduled separately on each device. They use generic text rather than financial amounts and are cancelled when the linked bet no longer needs review. Shortcuts open the calculator or bet list; any enabled app authentication still applies.

Exports and backup

CSV and full backup files can contain personal financial records and notes. Exporting is your choice. The app uses Apple’s sharing and file interfaces; the destination you choose determines who can access the file. Keep exported copies in a location you trust.

Retention and deletion

Records and revision history remain while you use the app. Recently Deleted is reversible recovery, not permanent erasure. Removing the app normally removes its local container, but does not necessarily remove device backups, exported files or a private iCloud copy. For help with complete removal, contact support. We cannot recover local-only records that you have deleted without a backup.

Support and website visits

If you choose to contact support, we use the information you submit to investigate and respond. GitHub support tickets are public. Do not include bet records, balances, backup files, account credentials or personal identifiers in a public ticket. Use a minimal redacted example; ask for a private channel when a sensitive issue needs one.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. We add no website analytics scripts, advertising pixels, tracking cookies or contact forms. GitHub may process technical request information to operate its hosting service under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Adults and changes

This app is intended for adults aged 18 and over. If the app’s features or data practices change, this policy will be updated with a new effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, access requests or deletion help, visit Matched Bets Calculator support. Do not post sensitive records publicly.
