A few things to know

Is the app free?

Yes. The current app is free to use, with no subscriptions, in-app purchases or advertising.

Does it place bets or connect my accounts?

No. This is a manual calculator and personal record keeper. You enter your own odds and actual matched amounts, and settle each record yourself.

Does it support iPhone and iPad?

Yes, on iOS and iPadOS 18 or later. Records support GBP and EUR, with separate totals for each currency.

What about iCloud?

Private iCloud sync is being prepared for release. The current internal beta saves locally; use the full backup export to keep an independent copy.

Are calculator estimates guaranteed?

No. Odds, unmatched bets, offer conditions, commission and provider settlement rules can change the result. Check both accounts and record actual settlement figures. Refund conversion estimates are promotional value, not cash already earned.
