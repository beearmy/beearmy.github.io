About AndyHQ

AndyHQ is the solo indie studio behind Overtime Live, a free real-time overtime tracker for shift workers on iOS and Android — built because over 85% of UK shift workers still track overtime manually, with spreadsheets or paper.

The studio also builds Sun Hours, a free solar panel kWh calculator; Outflow, a privacy-first subscription tracker; and Landlord Assistant: Rent, for landlords and lodgers. Every app follows one principle: solve one problem, solve it well, and respect your privacy.
